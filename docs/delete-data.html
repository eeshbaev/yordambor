Request data deletion — YordamBor

Last updated: 1 September 2026

This page explains how users of YordamBor can delete some or all personal data. You do not need to delete your whole account to remove specific information in many cases.

Delete data in the app (no account deletion)

  1. Open the YordamBor app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile (bottom navigation).
  3. Phone number: open Settings and turn off phone visibility, or edit your profile to remove your phone number from what others see.
  4. Service listings & photos: open your listing → manage/edit → remove portfolio images or delete the entire service post.
  5. Profile photo: update or remove your avatar in Profile settings.
  6. Local data on device: remove the app or clear app storage in Android settings to delete favorites and other data stored only on your phone.

Request deletion by email

To delete specific data you cannot remove in the app (for example deal messages, reviews, or uploaded files), email us:

eeshbaev@outlook.com

Include:

  • Your registered email address in YordamBor
  • What data you want deleted (be as specific as possible)

We respond within 30 days and confirm what was deleted.

Delete your entire account

To delete your account and associated personal data in one step:

  1. Open YordamBor → Profile → Settings
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm

After account deletion we remove or anonymize personal data within a reasonable period, except where law requires longer retention (for example fraud prevention or legal obligations).

What we delete vs keep

Data type Partial delete Account delete
Profile name, email, phone Edit in app / email request Deleted
Service listings & portfolio photos Delete in app Deleted
Deal messages & chat Email request Deleted
Reviews you wrote Email request Deleted or anonymized
Aggregated analytics / security logs Not individually identifiable May be retained briefly for security
