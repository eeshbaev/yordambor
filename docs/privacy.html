Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026 · App: YordamBor (Android & iOS)

YordamBor (“we”, “our”, “the app”) is a marketplace that connects service providers and clients in Uzbekistan. This policy explains what data we collect, why we use it, and your choices.

1. Data we collect

  • Account: name, email address, phone number, profile photo (optional), language preference
  • Services: service listings, portfolio photos, pricing, availability status, certificates
  • Deals & messages: deal requests, chat messages, appointment notes and photos you choose to attach
  • Device & usage: push notification tokens, basic in-app analytics (e.g. screen views), crash/diagnostic logs from the OS
  • Local data: favorites and client-book entries may be stored on your device

2. How we use data

  • Create and manage your account
  • Show your profile and services to other users
  • Run deals (Kelishuv) and send notifications
  • Keep the platform safe (reports, blocks, moderation)
  • Improve the app and fix errors

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Where data is stored

Account and app data are stored on encrypted servers operated by Supabase (hosting and database). Portfolio images are stored in Supabase Storage. Some data is cached locally on your phone.

4. Third-party services

  • Supabase — authentication, database, file storage
  • Google Play / Apple App Store — app distribution (subject to their policies)
  • Email delivery — account verification and password reset (via Supabase)

We only share data with partners required to run the app.

5. Permissions

The app may request access to:

  • Photos / camera — upload portfolio and deal photos
  • Notifications — deal updates and reminders
  • Internet — sync with our servers

6. Your rights

You can:

  • View and update your profile in the app (Settings)
  • Control whether your phone number is visible to others
  • Delete your account and associated data from Settings
  • Block users and report content

7. Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we remove or anonymize personal data within a reasonable period, except where law requires longer retention.

8. Children

YordamBor is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
